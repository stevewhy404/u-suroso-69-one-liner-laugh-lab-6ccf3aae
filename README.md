One short funny line answering a prompt: the excuse a robot vacuum gives after eating a sock.

> Your sock attacked my brush roll first. I defended myself, swallowed the evidence, and I would do it again.

`main.py` writes that line — exactly 107 characters, no trailing newline — to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (falling back to `/output`).
